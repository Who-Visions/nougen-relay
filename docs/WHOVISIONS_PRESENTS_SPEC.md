## 2. MONUMENTAL TYPOGRAPHY TOKENS
- **Display Sans**: Cabinet Grotesk / Clash Display / Syne (Fallback: system-ui, sans-serif 900)
- **Body & Interface**: Inter / Geist Sans
- **Scale**:
  - Hero Monument: 5.5rem - 8.0rem (88px - 128px), letter-spacing: -0.04em, line-height: 0.92
  - Campaign Headline: 3.5rem - 4.5rem (56px - 72px), letter-spacing: -0.03em, line-height: 0.95
  - Section Marker: 1.75rem - 2.25rem (28px - 36px), bold, uppercase, letter-spacing: -0.01em
  - Editorial Body: 1.125rem (18px), line-height: 1.55, regular / medium
  - Chip Annotation: 0.75rem (12px), semibold, uppercase, letter-spacing: 0.05em

---

## 3. HERO & EDITORIAL COMPONENT ARCHITECTURE
1. **Campaign Panel Hero**: Full-bleed responsive container with dark wine/burgundy underlay, high-contrast imagery, and layered declarative type blocks.
2. **Annotation Chips (Interface + Editorial Hybrid)**:
  - Floating pill geometry: border-radius: 9999px, frosted glass backdrop (backdrop-blur-md, bg-white/80 or bg-black/60).
  - Micro-indicator: Pulsing 6px dot + label + optional directional arrow.
3. **Monumental CTA System**:
  - Heavy rounded rectangle: h-14 to h-16, px-8, rounded-full or rounded-2xl.
  - Sovereign Crimson fill with crisp white typography, high tactile hover elevation.

---

## 4. RESPONSIVE CADENCE & MOBILE-FIRST RHYTHM
- **Header**: Minimalist top chrome (Who Visions mark left, sound/nav minimal right).
- **Stage 1 (Reveal)**: Monumental typography breaking across editorial subject matter.
- **Stage 2 (Lockup & CTA)**: Repeated brand mark anchor + oversized primary action button.
- **Stage 3 (Manifesto)**: Stark high-contrast proclamation (*WHO VISIONS PRESENTS premieres the universe*).
- **Stage 4 (Discovery Grid)**: Dual-column editorial commerce / narrative cards with annotation chips.
