### Doctrine
**HUMAN TIME RECOVERED IS THE METRIC.**

NouGen should not measure success only in prompts answered, tokens consumed, tasks completed, or API-equivalent compute. It should also measure how much human attention was released while useful work continued without loss of state.

### Architecture implication
When an agent drifts, the human should not have to reconstruct context manually. Point it back to shards. The agent should recover established decisions, routes, provenance, credentials doctrine, and prior failures, then resume execution. Relay carries work forward. Shards preserve institutional state. SSH/LAN and provider-native coordination let workers cooperate. The human becomes director rather than packet courier.

### Product / telemetry candidates
Explore first-class metrics such as operator-attention minutes, unattended execution duration, autonomous baton transitions, context-recovery events, drift-to-realignment latency, tasks completed while operator absent, and human-hours returned.

### Narrative
The computers were still working while the human baked a cake. That is the demo. AI should give people back pieces of their actual lives, not merely create another screen that demands continuous supervision.
