## Judging / voting mechanics — RESOLVED

Per maxim-flash.com, FLASH runs as:
1. **Entry Round** — 5 weeks, photographers in grouped public-vote matchups. Win your group to advance.
2. **Elimination Tournament** — semis, finals, decided by public vote.
3. **Final Judging** — finalists only. Judging panel reviews the *full profile*, not a single image, and selects the Flash Champion.

Free daily votes plus **supporters can buy additional votes**. Paid voting is the revenue engine. The on-page "purchases will NOT help entrant win" disclaimer is the standard AMOE line covering *entrant* purchases — it does not cover third-party vote purchases, which are the actual advancement lever.
