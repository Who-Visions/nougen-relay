## Matured Kirby Ferguson Protocol: from remix theory to recursive agent operations

Dave identifies his long-running Kirby Ferguson / Everything Is a Remix influence as having matured into an operational NouGen protocol.

### Source principle
Ferguson’s creative framework: **Copy → Transform → Combine.**

Dave’s mature implementation turns that creative observation into executable fleet verbs and a recursive engineering cycle.

### NouGen operational sequence

1. **DEEP GREP THAT SHIT**
   Investigate beyond surface retrieval. Search broadly, follow references and provenance, inspect neighboring evidence, reconcile contradictions, and recover the real lineage/context.

2. **REVERSE THAT SHIT**
   Work backward from outputs, artifacts, behavior, interfaces, and dependencies to recover the underlying mechanism and principles. The objective is understanding and reconstruction, not blind copying.

3. **TRANSFORM IT**
   Adapt recovered principles to Dave’s actual objective, constraints, architecture, and design language.

4. **COMBINE IT**
   Fuse useful mechanisms with NouGen, existing infrastructure, prior shards, other systems, and original ideas into a new implementation.

5. **PARALLELOGRAM IT**
   Where work is genuinely independent, decompose into concurrent lanes, execute with appropriate agents/tools, preserve provenance, then converge and validate.

6. **SHARD THAT SHIT**
   Persist the durable learning, decisions, failures, vocabulary, and why the result holds so future lanes do not rediscover the same knowledge from zero.

7. **RELAY THAT SHIT**
   Propagate actionable state and discoveries across the fleet so other machines/agents can immediately inherit and continue the work.

8. **RECURSE**
   Treat the transformed system and its newly captured knowledge as source material for the next cycle.

### Compressed loop
**SEE → DECOMPOSE → REMIX → BUILD → REMEMBER → PROPAGATE → REMIX AGAIN**
