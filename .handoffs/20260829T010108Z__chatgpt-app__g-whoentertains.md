### Important qualification
Do not measure 'human time returned' as raw agent runtime. That would inflate the number. It should estimate/manual-baseline time that the user would otherwise have needed to spend actively supervising, reconstructing context, copying state between tools, repeating searches, rerunning known fixes, or executing the task themselves. Any public metric needs a documented methodology and confidence band.

### Architecture implication
